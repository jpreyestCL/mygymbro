MyGymBro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

MyGymBro is a training log. It records what you lift and what you weigh, and it syncs that between your own devices. This page says exactly what is stored, where, and who can see it. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

What is collected

DataWhyWhere it lives
A display name So the app can greet you and tell profiles apart. Our server

Apple Health

If you allow it, MyGymBro reads your body weight from Health so you do not have to retype what your scale already recorded, and writes the weigh-ins you log back to Health so your other apps see them.

Body weight is the only data type requested. MyGymBro never reads workouts, heart rate, or anything else from Health. Health data is used solely to show you your own history inside the app: it is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with third parties. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app, and MyGymBro keeps working without it.

What is not collected

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting SDK or advertising identifiers.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • No passwords — sign-in is by passkey, so there is no password to store or leak.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.

Who can see your data

Your training log is visible to you. Whoever operates the server can technically access the files on it, as with any hosted service; it is not read, mined, or handed to anyone else. If the instance you use has an administrator dashboard enabled, an administrator can see account names and training history for the accounts on that instance — the app tells you when this applies.

Where it is stored

On a server in Germany (Hetzner), and in the app on your device. Data is sent over HTTPS. Your passkey's private key stays in your device's secure hardware or your password manager and is never transmitted.

Deleting your data

You can export everything from Settings at any time. To delete your account and all of its training data, use the delete option in Settings, or email the address below — deletion removes the account, its passkeys, and its training log from the server, and cannot be undone.

Children

MyGymBro is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, the app will say so before the change takes effect.
